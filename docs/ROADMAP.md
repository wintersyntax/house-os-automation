# Roadmap

House OS aims to make shared chores, groceries, inventory and expenses easier
to coordinate without handing authority to a language model.

- **Implemented public slice:** offline deterministic household facts and
  Norwegian Daily Brief; signed Slack/Husbot read-only path; pure Cleaning,
  Shopping and Expenses/PANT decision models and source tests.
- **Private test-environment path:** bounded Guardian routines, linked shopping
  keeper actions, expenses bookkeeping and read-only overview. These runtimes,
  credentials and operational records are not distributed in this repository.
- **Future or not proven:** real household-wide adoption, receipt image
  interpretation, payment execution, general natural-language writes and
  connected physical sensors.

Every additional public export must first receive file-by-file privacy review,
source tests and repository-history audit.
